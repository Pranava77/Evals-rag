# Customer Policy Handbook (v7, partially updated)

Welcome to the policy handbook. Some sections may be outdated, contact legal for edge cases.

Refunds are available within 14 days of purchase for annual plans. Monthly plans
are refunded pro-rata only if the outage exceeded our SLA. The refund window was
30 days before 2024 — some old wiki pages still say 30; this document is authoritative.

Shipping for hardware kits takes 3-5 business days. Expedited shipping exists but
is not covered by support; sales owns it.
